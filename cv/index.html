It also reached gold-level performance in another Kaggle competition translating 4,000-year-old Akkadian clay tablets into English, showing it can generalize across domains. [announcement]

    • Data Collection, Annotation & Vendor Management: Led large-scale data collection and preference annotation with external vendors, used for post-training of models and LLM judges to improve the long-horizon ideation, planning, and judging abilities of AIRA2.
