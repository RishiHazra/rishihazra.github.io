I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at **[Meta Superintelligence Labs](https://ai.meta.com/research/)** in London, working with [Yoram Bachrach](https://sites.google.com/view/yoram-bachrach) on AI Research Agents (AIRA). Before that, I completed **two research internships at Meta**: at FAIR London with [Nicolas Carion](https://www.nicolascarion.com/) (SAM 3, AI research agents), and at Reality Labs Research in Redmond with [Ruta Desai](https://rutadesai.github.io/) and [Akshara Rai](https://ai.meta.com/people/423502423531745/akshara-rai/) ([EgoTV](https://rishihazra.github.io/EgoTV/), ICCV 2023).

My PhD, supervised by [Luc De Raedt](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/people/lucderaedt) and [Pedro Zuidberg Dos Martires](https://pedrozudo.github.io/), focused on LLM agents that use search to reason and make decisions.

I completed my Master's in AI at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) in 2019, supervised by [Ambedkar Dukkipati](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~ambedkar/). I also worked on natural language understanding at [Amazon Alexa-AI](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/alexa), and on multi-agent reinforcement learning as a Research Associate in IISc's [Statistics and Machine Learning Group](https://sml.csa.iisc.ac.in/).
